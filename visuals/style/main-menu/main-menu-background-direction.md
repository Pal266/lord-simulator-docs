# Main Menu Background Visual Direction

## Approval Status

This document records an explicitly approved visual direction for the Lord Simulator main-menu background.

It is authoritative style guidance under `VISUAL_STANDARD.md`.

It does **not** approve any existing background image as a production asset. A concrete background image must still be separately reviewed and explicitly approved before it may be placed under `visuals/approved/` or referenced by an implementation specification.

## Visual Intent

The main-menu background should communicate the central campaign fantasy: the player begins with a sparse, harsh northern frontier and helps transform it into a developed and organized region with settlements, infrastructure, and fortified urban centers.

The scene should feel grounded, historical, cold-climate, and northern rather than high-fantasy or heroic-fantasy.

## Environment

The approved environment direction includes:

- conifer forests and dark evergreen vegetation;
- rocky terrain and exposed stone;
- rivers, lakes, estuaries, or similar northern waterways;
- distant mountains, with snow primarily on higher elevations rather than covering the entire landscape;
- cool northern atmospheric conditions such as mist, low cloud, cold air, and long-distance haze;
- habitable lower terrain with visible earth, grass, forest, roads, timber, and construction areas;
- restrained seasonal snow where appropriate, but not a permanently snow-covered world.

## Development Theme

The background should visibly support the idea of regional development.

Useful scene elements include:

- small or emerging settlements;
- timber construction sites;
- docks and river infrastructure;
- bridges and roads;
- cleared land and working areas;
- expanding villages or towns;
- a more developed fortified settlement or castle in the distance.

The composition should suggest progression from wilderness and limited infrastructure toward greater settlement density and development.

## Tone and Realism

The approved tone is:

- grounded medieval realism;
- restrained and serious;
- rugged rather than ornate;
- atmospheric rather than magical;
- cold northern colors balanced by selective warm natural light;
- believable materials, terrain, buildings, and construction.

The background should avoid exaggerated fantasy architecture, magical effects, oversized monumental castles, excessively decorative environments, or other imagery that makes the scene read as generic high fantasy.

## Composition

The main menu uses a vertical button stack on the left side of the screen.

The background composition should therefore:

- reserve sufficient visually quiet space on the left for menu controls;
- avoid placing the primary landscape focal point directly behind the buttons;
- place major settlement, river, construction, or fortified-development detail primarily toward the center and right;
- remain readable when cropped or scaled for standard widescreen and ultrawide displays;
- avoid essential visual information at extreme screen edges.

## Heraldry and Setting-Specific Symbols

The background should not contain a Stark flag, direwolf banner, or other prominent house heraldry as part of the current approved direction.

Heraldry may be introduced only if a future visual decision or specification explicitly requires it.

## UI Relationship

The background should remain separate from menu controls.

It must not bake button frames, button labels, menu text, hover states, or other interactive UI into the image.

The approved main-menu buttons are separate production assets and localized button text is expected to be rendered by the UI system.

## Production Asset Status

No background image is approved for production by this document.

The next production step is to create a high-resolution background that follows this direction, review that exact image, and explicitly approve it before promotion to `visuals/approved/`.
